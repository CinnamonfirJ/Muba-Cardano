MUBA BUSINESS MODEL & REVENUE DOCUMENTATION
===========================================

1. OVERVIEW
-----------
Muba is a multi-vendor marketplace platform. The site generates revenue through transaction-based fees from both customers and vendors. The model is designed to scale with transaction volume while providing a seamless checkout experience.

2. HOW THE SITE MAKES MONEY (COMPANY REVENUE)
---------------------------------------------
The platform (Muba) earns revenue from two primary sources during a transaction:

A. CUSTOMER SERVICE FEE (Customer-Side)
   - Scope: Applied to the total cart value.
   - Rule: If Cart Subtotal >= ₦1,000, a flat ₦100 fee is added.
   - Rule: If Cart Subtotal < ₦1,000, no fee is charged (₦0).

B. PLATFORM COMMISSION (Vendor-Side)
   - Scope: Deducted from the vendor's product price earnings.
   - Rule: 2.5% commission on all transactions.
   - Additional Rule: If Order Item >= ₦1,000, an additional ₦100 flat fee is deducted from the vendor.

Formula for Company Revenue (CR) per Transaction (T):
-----------------------------------------------------
If Transaction Amount (S) >= ₦1,000:
   CR = (S * 0.025) + ₦100 [Vendor Flat] + ₦100 [Customer Service Fee]

If Transaction Amount (S) < ₦1,000:
   CR = (S * 0.025)

3. HOW VENDORS MAKE MONEY
-------------------------
Vendors earn by selling products on the platform. Their net earnings are deposited into their Paystack subaccounts automatically.

Vendor Earnings Calculation (VE):
---------------------------------
VE = Product Price - Platform Commission - Paystack Processing Fee

Note: Vendors bear the Paystack processing fees (approximately 1.5% + ₦100 for local transactions), ensuring the platform's 2.5% remains net revenue.

4. KEY METRICS & CALCULATIONS
-----------------------------

A. COMPANY METRICS
   - Monthly Market Revenue (MMR): Sum of all Platform Commissions + Customer Service Fees in a 30-day period.
     Calculation: Σ (Platform_Fee_n + Service_Fee_n) for all n transactions in Month.
   - Daily Transaction Volume (DTV): Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) processed through the platform in 24 hours.
     Calculation: Σ (Total_Paid_by_Customer_n) for all n transactions in Day.
   - Total Revenue Earned: Cumulative platform earnings since launch.
     Calculation: Σ (Company_Revenue_All_Time).

B. VENDOR METRICS
   - Cumulative Vendor MMR: The combined monthly earnings of all vendors on the platform.
     Calculation: Σ (Vendor_Earnings_n) for all vendors in Month.
   - Total Vendor Revenue (TVR): Cumulative gross sales generated by all vendors.
     Calculation: Σ (Product_Price_n * Quantity_n) for all sales.

5. STAKEHOLDER BENEFITS
-----------------------

FOR CUSTOMERS:
- Secure Payments: Integrated with Paystack and Cardano for high-security transactions.
- Diverse Inventory: Access to multiple college-based vendors in one checkout.
- Lower Fees: Competitive shipping (school_post, peer_to_peer) and no service fees for small purchases (< ₦1,000).

FOR VENDORS:
- Exposure: Instant access to the college student market.
- Automated Payouts: No manual withdrawal requests; funds are split instantly to subaccounts.
- Fraud Protection: Verification systems ensure only genuine orders are processed.
- Analytics: Detailed order management and tracking through the vendor dashboard.
